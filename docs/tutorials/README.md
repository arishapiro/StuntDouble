# Stunt Double tutorials

Step-by-step guides to Stunt Double's interface, each with screenshots. Work through them in
order: each builds on the one before. Each is here as Markdown (read it on GitHub, or in any
Markdown viewer) and as a PDF (to read offline or print).

| | Tutorial | What you do | |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | [The Basics](tutorial-1-the-basics.md) | Drop a ragdoll, pose it (joints and IK), launch it, measure the damage, hang it by one hand | [PDF](tutorial-1-the-basics.pdf) |
| 2 | [Objects and Collisions](tutorial-2-objects-and-collisions.md) | Add a ball and a wall: shoot the ball at him, fall onto it, hold and throw it | [PDF](tutorial-2-objects-and-collisions.pdf) |
| 3 | [Controllers and the Timeline](tutorial-3-controllers-and-the-timeline.md) | Balance, pose, jump and hybrid controllers, and timed events on the timeline | [PDF](tutorial-3-controllers-and-the-timeline.pdf) |
| 4 | [IK, Motion Capture and Rendering](tutorial-4-ik-motion-capture-and-rendering.md) | Keep an IK pose, play motion capture, render a video and its control passes | [PDF](tutorial-4-ik-motion-capture-and-rendering.pdf) |
| 5 | [Paths and Actions](tutorial-5-paths-and-actions.md) | A learned robot walks an obstacle course: steer it with a path, script its actions on the timeline, kick a ball into a goal | [PDF](tutorial-5-paths-and-actions.pdf) |
| 6 | [Robot Fight](tutorial-6-robot-fight.md) | Two robots walk up to each other and trade punches; choreograph the fight and stage a knockout | [PDF](tutorial-6-robot-fight.pdf) |

The scenes they use are in the Scripts menu (`scripts/tutorials/`); the numbered tutorial
scripts there (Tutorial_01 to Tutorial_48) are separate example scenes, explained at the top of
each script. Tutorials 5 and 6 use two of them: Tutorial_47_Obstacle_Course and
Tutorial_48_Robot_Fight.
